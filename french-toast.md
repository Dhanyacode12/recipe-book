# French Toast

## Ingredients

- 4 slices of bread
- 2 eggs
- 120 ml milk
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- Butter for frying
- Maple syrup or honey to serve

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs, milk, and cinnamon in a shallow bowl.
2. Dip each bread slice into the mixture on both sides.
3. Fry in butter over medium heat until golden brown on each side.
4. Serve warm with maple syrup or honey.
