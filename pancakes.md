Pancakes are extremly delicious ! 

Try this pancake recipe. I am sure you will love it.

Ingredients

* 1 cup all-purpose flour
* 1 tablespoon sugar
* 1 teaspoon baking powder
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1 cup milk
* 1 egg
* 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
2. Add the milk, egg, and melted butter.
3. Stir until the ingredients are just combined. Do not overmix.
4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
5. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan.
6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip the pancake.
7. Cook the other side until golden brown.
8. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
